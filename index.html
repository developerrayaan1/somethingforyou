NOTES FROM A QUIET EVENING✧

For the moments that linger

Maybe it's the way the evening turns blue,

or the way a small moment stays;

how a flower can be just a flower,

and still brighten an ordinary day.

Some things don't ask to be understood,

they only ask to be seen—

like stars appearing one by one,

like a little patch of green.

— a thought
